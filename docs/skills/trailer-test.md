# Skill: trailer test

1. Focus a hero or card; the trailer must start after 5 s and reset when focus moves.
2. Sound is on after the first remote press or the Who's watching press; mute toggle and volume persist per profile.
3. Only one trailer plays at a time; it stops at once on focus leave, page change or player open.
4. No YouTube controls, title bar or end screen are visible; the trailer loops before the end.
5. If the embed fails, the next trailer is tried, then the backdrop stays.
6. Kill the network: the backdrop stays and nothing errors on screen.
