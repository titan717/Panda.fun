# Panda.fun v1.3.1

Signed release build for Android phones, tablets and Android TV.

## What's new in 1.3.1

- **A new TV launch intro: a retro paper-cutout film**: on TVs the app now opens with a stop-motion short drawn like cut paper, moving in the stepped 12 frames a second of hand-made animation, with boiling edges, film grain, scratches and a corner cue mark. Two glowing blue eyes with gold rims open in the dark, the picture irises in while the panda pops together piece by piece (headphones, tinted shades that catch a glint, a lopsided smirk and a bamboo stick), the camera zooms into its left eye and the picture fades onto the Who's watching? horizon. About 5 s, with no text. Phones and computers keep the v1.3.0 intro.
- **A score that follows the film**: each paper piece lands with a soft paper click, and the deep drum hit now lands as the camera reaches the eye. It still plays only when the device allows sound, and follows the app's sound-off setting and volume.
- **Still the same rules**: the intro cannot be skipped and ignores every key, tap and click; it plays only on a fresh start; it always ends on time (6 s on TVs at most, 3 s on phones); Reduce motion shows a still picture of the assembled panda instead; Lite mode leaves out the heavier effects (boiling edges, paper shadows, grain and the warm tint).
- **Fixes**: the paper shadows switch off once the camera is deep in the zoom, so a TV is never asked to draw a shadow many times the size of the screen. The film grain is drawn once and moved each frame instead of being regenerated, which keeps older TV boxes smooth.

Testing for this release was done in desktop Chromium with TV (1280 × 720, 1920 × 1080) and phone (360 to 820 px) layouts and remote-key simulation. It was not checked on a physical Android TV, and the intro sound was not heard on real TV speakers.
